## For Assignment Part 3

I had a long 1 hour conference planning the new website design, so we branched the codebase to experiment with it, I proposed many sources of inspiration and asked chatgpt what the limitations of the implementations were, how to overcome some limitations, and whether some ideas were doomed to begin with. After settling on the idea, I began to query it to generate all the structural components of the design, the central portfolio, and mainly spent alot of time tinkering and messing the with specifications of the the side bar using the Squared square design I proposed and polished with chatgpt.

Then I ran numerous tests on every feature to see whether it was desirable, whether it would feel sleek, and whether it would benefit the overall design. After testing the compatibility on several devices and iterating again, I would ask it to compress and optimize the code for any redundancies to move forward with the next feature. Using this method, I was able to make systematic progress on the design and iteratively advance one step at a time without worrying about the previous components and segments.

There was one issue I faced, which was not detecting the many things ChatGPT missed until too late and we built too much without building the proper and integrated foundation for it, which led to some bloat in the system. I then explored whether to keep going and patch the problems was better or to go back and refactor the system to accomodate the features we designed. In the end, I used both techniques for certain features, depending on whether the feature was integral to the website or not, for small features, small patches were more likely since reiterating the entire development cycle was not worth the trouble. But for translating arabic, for example, I had to go back and add new attributes on every components to ensure they allowed for arabic translation for all achievements and segments and components. 

I learned alot on how to develop software with AI in this assignment, as I had no prior experience to using AI before this, I am very new to AI and basically used it like google. This was a very fruitful experience.
